FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

GOOD QUESTIONS.
BETTER BUILDS.

Know what you’re getting into before you buy. Here are answers to common questions about custom gaming desktops, configured laptops, and quote requests.

Do you offer both desktop and laptop gaming systems?

Yes. Topp Dawg Gaming offers custom gaming desktop quotes and high-performance custom-configured gaming laptop options. Laptop configurations depend on compatible platforms and suppliers.

Can I pick my own parts?

Yes. Our Build a PC configurator lets you select sample desktop parts and compatible laptop concept options. Component options and estimates are illustrative; actual compatibility, available stock and prices are confirmed by quote.

How much does a custom gaming computer cost?

Cost depends on the graphics hardware, processor, memory, storage, case, cooling, and current availability. Send your budget and target games to request a tailored quote; no fixed prices are promised on this site.

Can you help me choose a PC for a specific game?

Yes. Tell us which games you play, your display resolution, preferred settings, and frame-rate goals so the proposed configuration can be aligned with your priorities.

Do you sell prebuilt gaming PCs?

The focus of this website is made-to-order desktop configurations and configurable laptop options. Ask if you have a specific ready-made system in mind; availability is not listed here.

How long will my order take?

Timing varies by component and supplier availability and the type of system. Any estimated assembly, configuration, shipping, or pickup timeline should be confirmed in your quote before purchase.

Do you offer nationwide U.S. shipping and local Colorado pickup?

We serve Colorado customers and take quote inquiries for shipping across the United States. Enter your destination ZIP code; local handoff or pickup may be available by arrangement. Delivery eligibility, cost, and timing are confirmed before purchase.

What warranty or return policy applies?

Warranty, returns, support, and coverage details depend on the system and supplied components. Request the applicable written terms with your quote and review them before placing an order.

Does submitting a quote request place an order?

No. Requesting a quote is an inquiry only and does not create an order or payment obligation.

Can you calculate my shipping cost before I order?

Yes, request a tailored quote and include the destination state and ZIP code. Actual cost depends on package size, destination, carrier options, and selected protection services.

What if my PC is damaged in shipping?

Ask for the proposed packaging, carrier claim process, return instructions, and any optional coverage details in writing before approving an order. Do not assume any specific insurance or return policy applies until confirmed.

Can I visit your Colorado workshop?

Do not assume walk-in access. Any in-person meeting, pickup, or delivery arrangement must be confirmed directly before visiting.
