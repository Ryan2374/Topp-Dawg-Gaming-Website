TOPP DAWG GAMING - STRUCTURED SHOPPING PRICE SEARCH

The previous Chrome capture is now functioning, but Google Shopping blocked
headless access and Bing returned zero useful cards. This update switches the
automatic price search to SerpApi's structured Google Shopping results.

No supplier or reseller accounts are required. SerpApi has a free tier, but
REQUIRES a free search-service account and API key (currently 250 searches/month).
Visit https://serpapi.com/ to create a search-data account. Your key is NEVER
placed in site HTML or JavaScript, and is encrypted with Windows DPAPI.

1) Apply this update to your original website via APPLY_UPDATE.bat.
2) Run SETUP_SHOPPING_API.bat from the updated original website folder.
3) Paste your SerpApi key (the prompt hides it).
4) Restart start_local_test.bat. In lab.html select a part and click
   Search Shopping Prices Automatically.
5) Run DIAGNOSE_SHOPPING.bat for network/quota feedback if needed.

The markup on every retrieved matching part remains 20% for low-price parts,
sliding to 5% at $1,000. Listings are estimates only. Availability and final
checkout price cannot be confirmed by Google Shopping results.

Important: product identifiers in the early sample catalog are NOT exact
manufacturer part numbers for many parts, and some conceptual products cannot
be reliably priced. Keep unverified listings out of purchase-ready inventory.
The optional URL/manual capture UI may still exist; this provider does not
require those fields for automatic search.

The local server remains loopback-only. No public website or payment features
are enabled by this update. This is a testing integration, not live ordering.
