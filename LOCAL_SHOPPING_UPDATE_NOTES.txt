TOPP DAWG GAMING - BROWSER SHOPPING SEARCH (LOCAL TEST)

Start your website using start_local_test.bat, then open:
http://127.0.0.1:8765/lab.html

Select a specific component and click Search Shopping Prices Automatically.
The local server starts a separate temporary Edge/Chrome browsing session,
checks Bing Shopping and Google Shopping search results, and tries to identify
listings from Newegg, B&H, Micro Center and Best Buy. It does not need URLs.
If a listing exposes a USD price and model-family match, the site applies
20% down to 5% markup based on item cost and labels it SEARCH ESTIMATE.
It cannot verify current stock, tax, shipping, precise product variants,
or the price at final checkout. The search engine can block automation.
A blocked/empty result is NOT replaced with a fictitious sample price.
Generic case, cooler and concept laptop entries need actual model numbers.

Saved retailer page checks and shopping search observations are separate.
Existing site-settings.json, build.py, parts-catalog.json and saved prices
were preserved. Installer backups are in .tdg-update-backups.
Nothing is published online. Requires Windows 10/11 and Edge or Chrome.
If your original build.py overwrites pages, apply this update again afterward.
