YOUR INFORMATION

PRIVACY MATTERS.

This notice explains how inquiry information is handled through this starter website.

Effective date: November 19, 2026

Information you provide

When you contact Topp Dawg Gaming, you may voluntarily provide your name, email, optional phone number, destination state and ZIP code, delivery preferences, budget, requested system specifications, and details about your gaming or computing needs.

How requests are sent

Until an online form provider is configured, the quote form uses your own email application to prepare a message. You review and send that message yourself. This site does not independently deliver or store form submissions on a server.

How information is used

Information you send may be used to answer questions, discuss components and availability, and prepare requested estimates. Do not include sensitive financial information or payment card details in inquiry emails.

Hosting and third parties

The website host and your email provider may process technical information needed to deliver the site and messages. Their handling is governed by their own policies. This initial website does not include advertising trackers or third-party analytics scripts.

Data inquiries

For requests about information you sent to us, email support@toppdawggaming.com. This notice should be reviewed and updated before launch, including any later additions of payment, analytics, customer accounts, or an online contact-form service.
