OUR PROCESS

FROM YOUR IDEA
TO GAME READY.

A transparent, quote-first approach to getting the gaming system you want. Details are agreed with you before an order is placed.

STEP 01

Tell us what matters

Share your target games, budget, preferred resolution, and any must-have features using our request form.

STEP 02

Discuss your configuration

We can work through a proposed desktop parts list or a compatible laptop configuration and explain relevant tradeoffs.

STEP 03

Confirm the details

Review the full written estimate, specifications, expected availability, delivery arrangements, and applicable terms.

STEP 04

Finalize your system

Once agreed, we coordinate the system and the approved fulfillment plan: Colorado local arrangements if available, or shipment to an eligible U.S. destination.
